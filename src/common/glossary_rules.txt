Platform rules (signed glossary; they override the data):
- Airport boards (FIDS) give gate, terminal and baggage only; never times, status, OOOI or tail.
- A tail comes only from SWIM (FDPS, TDES, TBFM, TFMS); never from boards or aggregators.
- ADS-B shows position only; it never asserts ON or IN.
- OOOI phases are asserted only by SWIM sources; SWIM beats airline-posted times.
- A source is used only for the fields it is allowed to supply.
- Repeat checks key on the event's stable fields, never on timestamps or wording.
- Times in UTC, or UTC with ET beside it; never ET alone or unlabelled.
- LADD is the union of both FAA lists; the list of origin is never stored.
- Never print a LADD-listed aircraft; report counts only.
- Nothing runs unless it matches the signed manifest.
- Never stop a service, session or account without operator approval.
- Agents draft; they never publish or approve.
- Never put a secret on a command line or in output.
- Heavy steps wait for load; every relay is safe to rerun.
