## 3. Independent COGS / sale-price derivation

**ASSUMPTION (rate):** $100–175/hr blended senior-engineer rate, i.e.
~$12.5k–17k per fully-loaded person-month. All labor-derived figures
scale linearly with whatever rate the founder actually blesses.

Three methods, triangulated. No single-number precision is claimed.

**Method 1 — as-built cost floor.** 629 commits, single author, ~10.5
elapsed weeks at high intensity with heavy AI-assisted tooling ≈ **3–4
person-months as actually built** ⇒ **~$45k–70k**. This is the true
historical cost of goods and the floor of any honest sale price: no
rational seller prices below it, and it is low precisely because of AI
leverage a buyer cannot assume they'd replicate.

**Method 2 — conventional replacement ceiling.** ~59k LOC of delivered,
integrated code across 17 subsystems: a SWIM push client with
FIXM/ITWS/STDDS parsing, an SDR RF chain, a 21-model local-LLM
pipeline with integrity-gated builds, ~130 REST routes, a PWA, tiered
auth, and a semantic knowledge-graph layer. At an industry-typical
1,000–1,500 delivered LOC/person-month for integration-heavy systems ⇒
**~18–30 person-months ⇒ ~$225k–500k**. This is what it would cost a
conventional team to recreate — a *replacement value ceiling*, not a
sale price: the market discounts single-author code with no CI, no
multi-tenancy, no DR, and open licensing/IP paperwork (all still true
per the 08-09 reverification's open items) well below full replacement.

**Method 3 — market posture check.** Comparable commercial signals from
COST_STRUCTURE §2: single-feed enterprise data subscriptions alone run
~$500–$1,200/yr *per feed type*, and this asset internalizes ADS-B +
ACARS/VDL + SWIM + weather + cross-referencing with permanent data
ownership plus a local-inference intelligence layer on ~$1–2k of
hardware. That supports pricing the asset meaningfully above its
as-built floor, while the productization gaps (§2 note above) cap it
well under the replacement ceiling.

## 4. Reconciliation with the prior $65k–$100k estimate

The prior band's methodology is unrecoverable (§1), but it is closely
consistent with Method 1 + a modest premium applied to the ~08-09-era
codebase (~42k LOC, 16 models, no knowledge graph/semantic layer,

graph/semantic subsystem and integrity/hardening work**) yields

the §3 triangulation. Two routes, one answer:

> **⚠️ [2026-08-19] "Two routes, one answer" is circular.** §1 of this
> document establishes that the $65k–$100k band has **no recoverable
> methodology** — searched across 629 commits, 49 docs, the
> investor-materials copies and the vault, and not found. §4 then
> corroborates the new band by taking that same unmethodological prior and
> multiplying it by observed scope growth. **Scaling an unknown does not
> make it a second, independent measurement of the same quantity.** The
> "two routes" share one ancestor, so their agreement carries no
> confirmatory weight, and presenting it as convergence overstates
> confidence.
>
> The scaling arithmetic is also selectively favourable: **+40 % LOC ✅
> reproduces** (~42k → ~59k raw), and **16 → 21 models = +31 % ✅**, but
> $65k–$100k × 1.40 = **$91k–$140k** — the doc prints **$85k–$140k**,
> i.e. it applies +31 % to the bottom and +40 % to the top. Consistent
> scaling of both endpoints by the same factor is what "scaling by scope
> growth" means. Applying +31 % to both gives $85k–$131k; +40 % to both
> gives $91k–$140k. Neither is the printed band.
